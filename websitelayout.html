Jantar Mantar protest updates: Wangchuk breaks 26-day fast; relieved, says CJP founder Dipke, but vows to continue protest till Pradhan resigns.

Activist Sonam Wangchuk has broken his fast, announced Leh Apex Body co-convenor Cherring Dorjay Lakruk late on Thursday (July 23, 2026). Mr. Wangchuk broke the fast in the presence of two Union Ministers on certain conditions. The government gave a written assurance that cases against students will be taken back if they are not violent. The government also assured that compensation for NEET aspirants would be considered.
